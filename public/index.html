Both are reliable. The right one depends on how your team already works.

Questions people ask

Will I own the domain and accounts?

Yes. The domain, Google or Microsoft account and HubSpot account are all in your name and billed to you. We're added as an admin to do the setup, and you can remove us anytime.

Why do my emails go to spam?

Usually because three settings on your domain, called SPF, DKIM and DMARC, are missing or wrong. They prove to Gmail and Outlook that mail really comes from you. Our Email Health Check shows which ones you're missing.

Can you move my existing emails?

Yes. Email Launch includes moving your old mailboxes, for example from a Gmail address or your web host's email, into the new accounts.

What will I pay every year after setup?

The domain renewal and your Google or Microsoft licences, paid directly to them. The Care Plan is optional. We'll give you the exact figures for your setup before you start.

I already have a website. Can you just fix the email?

Yes. Email Launch works with any existing website and domain. We only change the email settings.
