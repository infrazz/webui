Questions people ask

Will I own the domain and accounts?

Yes. The domain, Google or Microsoft and Stripe accounts are all in your name and billed to you. We're added as an admin to do the setup, and you can remove us anytime.

Can you set up card and PayNow payments?

Yes. The Business package gives you a Stripe account in your business's name. Customers pay by card or PayNow, and Stripe pays out to your bank account. Stripe charges you a small fee per payment; we don't take a cut.

What is Cyber Essentials?

A cybersecurity mark from the Cyber Security Agency of Singapore (CSA), made for smaller businesses. It shows customers you have the basics in place. We get you ready; a CSA-appointed certification body does the assessment, and CSA funding may cover part of the fee for your first certification.

Do you do accounting, payroll or company registration?

No. Those belong with your accountant and corporate secretary. We work alongside them: for example, making sure your invoices, payments and email are set up the way your accountant needs.

Why do my emails go to spam?

Usually because three settings on your domain, called SPF, DKIM and DMARC, are missing or wrong. They prove to Gmail and Outlook that mail really comes from you. Our Email Health Check shows which ones you're missing.

Can you move my existing emails?

Yes. Every package includes moving your old mailboxes, for example from a Gmail address or your web host's email, into the new accounts.

What will I pay every year after setup?

The domain renewal and your Google or Microsoft licences, paid directly to them. The Care Plan is optional. We'll give you the exact figures for your setup before you start.

I already have a website. Can you just fix the email?

Yes. The Starter package works with any existing website and domain. We only change the email settings.
